Excuse Generator

Privacy Policy

app.pixora.excuses

Excuse Generator does not collect your personal data. It has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and it makes no network requests of its own.

What the app stores

Everything the app remembers is written to private storage on your own device and never leaves it. For Excuse Generator that is nothing beyond your language and theme choice, together with your language and appearance choices.

This data is not readable by other apps, is not transmitted anywhere, and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Purchases

Excuse Generator offers an optional Pro upgrade, sold either as a monthly subscription or as a one-time purchase. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play. The app never sees, handles or stores your payment details; it only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the upgrade, and remembers the yes-or-no answer on the device so Pro keeps working offline.

Google Play’s handling of a purchase is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. You can review or cancel a subscription at any time under Subscriptions in the Google Play app.

Permissions

  • Billing. Required by the Google Play Billing library so the Pro upgrade can be offered and restored.
  • Internet and network state. Declared by Google’s own billing and delivery libraries, which the app includes in order to sell the Pro upgrade. Excuse Generator itself performs no network requests and sends no data anywhere.

Deleting your data

Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete everything on this device. This clears everything the app has saved, immediately and permanently. Uninstalling the app removes it as well. Because nothing is ever uploaded, there is no server-side copy to request the deletion of.

Children

Excuse Generator is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children, collects no personal information from anyone, and contains no advertising, no user-generated content and no communication features.

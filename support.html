Purchases and refunds

Purchases are handled by Google Play, not by us. To see, cancel or request a refund for a purchase, open the Google Play app, then Menu → Subscriptions, or visit your Google Play subscriptions. If the Pro upgrade does not appear after paying, open the app, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases.

Deleting your data

Every app has Settings → Delete my data. Nothing is stored anywhere except your own phone.
